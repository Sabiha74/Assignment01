Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before attending DevConf 2026.

Where is the conference being held?

DevConf 2026 will take place at the International Convention Center, Dhaka, Bangladesh.

What's included in my ticket?

Your ticket includes access to all conference sessions, networking events, coffee breaks, conference materials, and a digital certificate.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. You can request a full refund up to 14 days before the event. After that, tickets are transferable but non-refundable.

Will the sessions be recorded?

Yes. All registered attendees will receive access to the session recordings after the conference.

Is food provided?

Lunch, coffee, tea, and snacks will be available throughout the conference for all attendees.

How can I contact the organizers?

You can reach our support team anytime at support@devconf2026.com.
